## About Yami Collection

Yami Collection describes itself as a **Gurgaon-based family startup inspired by the founders’ daughter, Yami**. Its [About Us page](https://yamicollection.com/about-us/) presents the business as a friendly online mini-mall: a curated selection of practical, colourful and giftable products for children and homes.

Unlike a specialist store with only one category, Yami Collection covers several common shopping occasions:

- **School and everyday carry:** school bags, lunch bags, lunch boxes, organisers, pouches and travel accessories.
- **Drinkware and meals:** tumblers, bottles, sippers, insulated food jars and lunch containers.
- **Stationery and activity:** essential stationery, educational items, art and craft products, games and toys.
- **Accessories and gifting:** hair accessories, gifts, selected jewellery, seasonal products and festive collections.
- **Lifestyle and home items:** a rotating selection of practical products and small gifts for home and daily use.

The company's public contact details list **Sector 84, Gurgaon, Haryana – 122004**, telephone **+91 98296 25112** and **contact@yamicollection.com**. Product availability, seasonal collections and specific charges can change and should be checked against the live store at purchase time.

## The Challenge

A broad lifestyle catalogue offers convenience, but it can become difficult to navigate. Someone shopping for a school lunch box is not necessarily interested in jewellery; a parent looking for a creative gift may not know the brand or product name; and a returning buyer of insulated bottles may want to compare capacity, colour and delivery availability immediately.

The website therefore needs more than a long grid of products. It must **separate product families, make browsing approachable, show meaningful options, and answer delivery and return questions before checkout**. Colourful product imagery and seasonal merchandise should support discovery without overwhelming the navigation or hiding essential buying details.

These are the practical ecommerce needs indicated by the business and its live website. They are **not** an independently measured audit of an older Yami Collection website.

## How the Website Presents the Shopping Experience

The observations below describe features visible on the public site and the documented Tech Conversion web-development engagement. They should not be read as a complete internal development log or proof that every checkout integration has passed an end-to-end test.

1. **Introduce the online mini-mall through recognisable categories.** The homepage opens with visual shortcuts for bottles and tumblers, lunch boxes, school bags, stationery, accessories, gifting, festive products, kids’ clothing and jewellery.
2. **Make a diverse catalogue manageable.** The [Shop](https://yamicollection.com/shop/) offers category, price, rating, colour/theme and stock-status filters alongside product cards and sorting tools. These are observable interface controls; filter behaviour has not been independently regression-tested.
3. **Give customers several ways to discover products.** Homepage modules include **Latest**, **Return Gifts** and **Best Sellers**, alongside featured products, seasonal or discounted selections and a [New Arrivals](https://yamicollection.com/new-arrivals/) page.
4. **Show actual product options.** Selected product-detail pages offer colour variants, price, quantity controls and an **Add to cart** action. For example, the [Sitarayuri Insulated Tumbler](https://yamicollection.com/product/sitarayuri-insulated-tumbler/) lists a colour selector and delivery information.
5. **Add location-aware delivery information.** Some product pages include a six-digit pincode checker and published dispatch/shipping estimates. These are seller-provided estimates; current delivery serviceability and checkout charges need confirmation.
6. **Communicate important purchase terms.** The site's current promotional bar advertises India-wide delivery, free shipping above **₹999**, and cash on delivery with a **₹70 additional charge**. These are client-published terms and should be checked against the current checkout and policy pages before reuse.
7. **Make gifting a meaningful shopping route.** Gifts, return-gift selections, character-themed accessories and special-event collections give shoppers alternatives to searching for a specific product name.
8. **Provide a direct contact and support path.** The [Contact Us](https://yamicollection.com/contact-us/) page lists the shop's Gurgaon location, support email, telephone number and enquiry form; the storefront also shows a WhatsApp help route.
9. **Support a friendly consumer-brand identity.** The About page connects the store's name with its family origin and explains why its catalogue combines everyday-use products, creative children's items and giftable discoveries.
10. **Connect discovery to purchasing without making unsupported claims.** Cart buttons, variant selectors and product pages are visible; payment-gateway security, order fulfilment and live checkout completion were not independently audited for this case study.

## Quality Standards to Review

These are **appropriate review criteria**, not claims that a formal audit or certification was completed.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** assess catalogue images, category navigation, visual stability, mobile loading and product-page responsiveness.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** test accessible menus, price and colour filters, variant labels, cart actions, keyboard navigation and image descriptions.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** check discoverable product/category content, descriptive titles, logical internal links and avoidance of duplicate or thin filter pages.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** review accurate Product, Offer, BreadcrumbList and Organisation structured data where supported by published information.
- **Product accuracy:** verify capacities, dimensions, materials, colours, character licensing where relevant, availability and product photos against the goods actually supplied.
- **Consumer terms and returns:** align promotional shipping text with checkout charges, cash-on-delivery fees, applicable consumer-protection obligations and the published refund/return policy. Product pages also mention an unedited parcel-opening video for certain claims; review that condition for clarity and legal compliance.
- **Payments and privacy:** verify actual payment-provider handling, HTTPS, safe storage of contact/order details, and secure handling of checkout, address and WhatsApp information.
- **Claims and testimonials:** verify permission and evidence for customer quotations, delivery statements, safety claims, product performance or promotional savings before presenting them as audited facts.
